Key Clinical Takeaways

Summary
  • Global Priority: Causes 505,268 annual deaths despite a 50.9% drop since 1990.
  • Infant Vulnerability: 5× higher detection in infants ≤1 yr (110.5 vs. 21.6/100k) and 75% IPD fatality.
  • Smart Prescribing: Avoid macrolide monotherapy in Egypt (~58% resistance); use high-dose amoxicillin for CAP.
  • Prevention Impact: PCV13 covers ~74% of dominant Egyptian serotypes and curbs carriage (~30%).
Note: Egyptian figures reflect hospital-based & systematic review studies (not national surveillance). References: 1. GBD 2021 LRI Study | 2. Draz et al. (2015) Cairo Univ. | 3. El-Kholy et al. (2020) | 4. CLSI M100
